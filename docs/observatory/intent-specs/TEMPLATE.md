## Intent statement

One sentence naming the beneficiary and observable outcome.

## Problem and evidence

State the present problem, verified evidence, urgency, and how completion will
be recognized. Separate known evidence from assumptions.

## Primary users and decisions

| User | Decision supported |
| --- | --- |
| Role | Concrete decision |

## Scope

List included entities, sources, time periods, outputs, interfaces, and evidence
classes.

## Explicit exclusions

List claims, features, sources, uses, and decisions that this work must not
support.

## Success measures

Number pass/fail outcomes. Include benchmark, coverage, reproducibility,
latency, safety, and explanation measures where applicable.

## Semantics

Define what high, low, missing, and suppressed results mean. List permitted and
prohibited interpretations.

## Data boundaries

Define source, rights, privacy, retention, access, publication, and deletion
requirements without assuming that public availability grants reuse rights.

## Constraints

State cost, performance, reproducibility, explainability, technology, and
operational limits.

## Dependencies

Separate approval prerequisites from coordination interfaces, downstream
consumers, execution specs, shared contracts, and external decisions. A
coordination interface is not an approval prerequisite unless explicitly
promoted and the index is updated.
